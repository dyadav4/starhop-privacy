StarHop Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

The short version

  • StarHop does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no tracking.
  • StarHop does not connect to the internet and does not send any data to us or to anyone else.
  • Your child's progress (stickers, badges and quiz stars) is saved only on your device.
  • The camera is used only in the optional "In My Room" mode to show planets in your room. Nothing is recorded or saved.

Who we are

StarHop is a space-learning app for children, made by Dharamvir Kumar Yadav. This policy explains what happens to information when you or your child use StarHop on iPhone or iPad.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. StarHop does not ask for names, email addresses, photos, location, contacts, or any other personal details. We do not use analytics tools, advertising networks, or any third-party services that collect data.

Information saved on your device

To remember your child's progress, StarHop saves a small amount of information on the device itself:

  • Which places in the app have been visited (the sticker collection)
  • Badges earned and quiz stars
  • Settings such as voice, music and sound effects on or off

This information never leaves the device through StarHop. It may be included in your device's normal backups (for example, iCloud Backup), which are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. You can erase it at any time with Reset all progress in the app's grown-ups area, or by deleting the app.

Camera

The optional "In My Room" mode uses the camera to place a 3D planet in your surroundings (augmented reality). The app will ask for permission before using the camera, and the rest of StarHop works fully without it. The camera picture is processed on the device only while this mode is open. StarHop does not take photos or videos, and nothing from the camera is stored or sent anywhere.

You can turn camera access on or off at any time in the device's Settings → StarHop.

Voice

StarHop reads text aloud using the voice built into iPhone and iPad. This happens on the device. StarHop does not use the microphone and never listens to or records anyone.

Children's privacy

StarHop is designed for children aged 6 and up. Because we do not collect any personal information, we do not collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We follow the rules for apps in Apple's Kids Category. Settings and other grown-up features are kept behind a simple parental gate.

Links and purchases

StarHop contains no advertising and no in-app purchases. If we ever add links that leave the app, they will be placed behind the parental gate.

Changes to this policy

If we change how StarHop handles information, we will update this policy and change the effective date above. If a change would mean collecting any information, we will update the app's privacy details on the App Store before it happens.
